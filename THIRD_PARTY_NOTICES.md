# Third-party notices

Apart from the CC0 photoscans listed below, CAUSEWAY ships no third-party art. Every mesh, texture and animation in
`public/assets` is generated by the Blender scripts in `blender/`, except the
runner's source mesh and textures, which were supplied by the project owner —
see `blender/sources/runner/PROVENANCE.md`.

Fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and bundled from npm: Bungee (`@fontsource/bungee`), Marcellus SC (`@fontsource/marcellus-sc`) and Alegreya Sans (`@fontsource/alegreya-sans`). Interface frame art in `public/ui/` is rendered by `blender/build_ui.py`.

## Photoscanned materials (Poly Haven, CC0 1.0)

These scans are bake inputs: `tools/fetch-polyhaven.mjs` downloads their 2K maps into
`blender/cache/polyhaven/` (not committed; treated as data only), the kit bake projects them onto
the stones, pavers and boards, and the game ships only the baked atlases. `src/world/detail_stone.webp`
is a 1K tile derived from Rock Face (normal map and cavity) for the near-camera detail normals.
Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), https://polyhaven.com/license.
